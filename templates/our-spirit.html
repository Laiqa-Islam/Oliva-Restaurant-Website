Oliva begins with an appetite for the simple things. A tomato at its sweetest. Dough that has had time to rest. The first crackle from a hot pan.

We cook in the spirit of the Mediterranean: with the season, for the table, and with enough to pass around. The food is thoughtful. The evening can take care of itself.

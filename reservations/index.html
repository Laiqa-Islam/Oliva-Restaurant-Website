Before you settle in

What happens after I fill in the form?+

You’ll see a preview of your chosen date, time, and party size. You can download a calendar reminder or change the details. This concept does not send a booking request.

Can I preview a table for a group?+

The form supports parties of one to six. Choose your party size alongside your date and time. Larger groups would need an arrangement with a real restaurant.

Can I include accessibility or dietary needs?+

Use the notes field to try adding an occasion, accessibility needs, or dietary requirements. These notes stay in the page and are not sent to anyone.

Why can’t I select some times?+

Available choices follow the sample opening hours. Mondays, past dates, and times outside service are unavailable.
